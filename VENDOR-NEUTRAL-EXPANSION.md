# Vendor-Neutral Expansion / Breadcrumb Principle

## Purpose

Rabbit Hole is an independent prototype and optional continuation of a broader creative trail:

**Inpatient Corridors → Halloween event → Yellow Door → Rabbit Hole → optional artifacts**

This is an expansion of the design space, not a pitch to replace an existing website team, agency, developer, designer, label partner, or other vendor.

The prototype is intended to be discoverable without creating an obligation to engage with it.

## Vendor neutrality

Rabbit Hole must not:

- imply that an existing creative or technical vendor is inadequate;
- imply that an artist should change vendors;
- present the prototype as a procurement proposal;
- use the project to pressure a rights holder into a response;
- imply endorsement, approval, partnership, or adoption from a person or organization merely because their work is referenced;
- treat a breadcrumb, link, or discovery path as a request for access.

The implementation is deliberately portable so that any future team could understand the concept without being required to use this repository, its stack, its commerce layer, or its author.

## Existing relationships are not inferred

The repository does not know or assume the current contractual, retainer, agency, management, label, design, or development relationships of any referenced artist or rights holder.

If an existing vendor relationship is in place, this prototype does not ask the rights holder to displace it.

## Breadcrumb, not pressure

The desired experience is:

**encounter → curiosity → optional exploration → choice**

not:

**encounter → sales pitch → vendor comparison → pressure to act**

The Yellow Door is a story mechanism first. Commerce, if present, remains optional and clearly separated from any claim about artistic intent or affiliation.

## Rights and attribution

AI assistance and project interpretation do not establish artist intent, permission, affiliation, endorsement, licensing, ownership, or authorship.

Before public or commercial use, human review must confirm applicable permissions and ensure that copy, links, media, product references, and attribution cannot reasonably be read as an endorsement or partnership.

## Human choice

A future rights holder or creative team may review, ignore, adapt, reject, redirect, or rebuild any part of this concept. The project should leave that decision entirely open.
